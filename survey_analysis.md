Groww Investor Voice — Primary Survey Analysis
1. Research overview
Research method: Online questionnaire
Platform: Google Forms
Collection date: 9 November 2026
Total responses: 10
Consent obtained: 10
Eligible respondents: 6
Ineligible respondents: 4
2. Eligibility criteria

Respondents had to:

Agree to participate.
Have started their first-ever mutual fund SIP.
Have started within the last 12 months.

Six respondents met these criteria.

Four were excluded from the primary analysis because they either had previous SIP experience or had started their first SIP more than 12 months ago.

3. Current SIP status

Among six eligible respondents:

Status	Count	Percentage
Continuing	4	66.7%
Paused	1	16.7%
Discontinued	1	16.7%

These are self-reported current statuses, not measured 12-month retention outcomes.

7. SIP stopping consideration
Response	Count
Yes	1
No	2
Prefer not to say	3

The sample is too small to draw conclusions about stopping behaviour.

The conditional follow-up question also received responses from some participants who answered No or Prefer not to say. Those answers should not be treated as reliable stopping-trigger evidence.

8. Emerging hypotheses
First-time investors may need clearer explanations of investment performance.
Transaction and deduction transparency may reduce operational confusion.
Beginner-friendly terminology may improve comprehension.
Financial commitments may affect the ability to maintain SIPs.
Investors may depend on external sources when app explanations are insufficient.

These hypotheses require interview validation.

9. Research limitations
Only ten survey responses were received.
Only six respondents met the target-segment criteria.
This was a convenience sample, not a representative sample.
Responses were self-reported.
No internal Groww customer data was collected.
No causal relationship with SIP continuation has been established.
The survey did not establish whether participants used Groww or another investment platform.
10. Research decision

The next research stage will involve qualitative interviews with eligible first-time SIP investors.

Priority should be given to understanding:

The paused participant's experience.
The discontinued participant's experience.
The experiences of continuing participants.
The differences between transaction confusion, investment understanding and financial flexibility.

Research integrity: All findings in this report are based on the ten actual survey responses supplied for analysis. Percentages use six eligible respondents unless otherwise stated.
