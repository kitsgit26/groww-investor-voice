3. Scoring Framework

Each opportunity will be rated from 1 to 5.

Criterion	Weight
Frequency	15%
Severity	20%
User impact	20%
Influence on SIP continuation	20%
Evidence strength	10%
Product feasibility	10%
Differentiation	5%

Weighted score formula:

Sum of (Rating / 5 × Criterion Weight).
