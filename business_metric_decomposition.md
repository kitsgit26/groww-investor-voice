Groww Investor Voice — Business Metric Decomposition
1. Business Objective

Increase the percentage of first-time mutual fund investors who still have an active SIP 12 months after starting their first SIP.

2. North Star Metric

12-month Active SIP Continuation Rate.

Formula

(Number of eligible first-time investors with an active SIP at month 12 / Total eligible first-time investors in the cohort) × 100.

3. Metric Definitions
Denominator

All eligible first-time mutual fund investors whose first successful SIP instalment occurred 12 months before the observation date.

Numerator

Eligible investors with at least one active SIP that has had a successful instalment during the 30 days preceding the month-12 observation date.

Active SIP

At least one successful instalment in the last 30 days.

Cohort eligibility
Investor is a first-time mutual fund investor.
First successful SIP instalment is identifiable.
Investor has reached the 12-month observation point.
4. Metric Tree
North Star

12-month Active SIP Continuation Rate.

Potential contributing dimensions
Successful first SIP instalment.
Subsequent instalment success.
Payment failure recovery.
User understanding of transaction status.
Understanding of NAV and investment terminology.
Realistic expectations about investment performance.
Confidence and trust.
Financial flexibility.
Ability to resolve questions independently.

These dimensions are hypotheses and require validation.

5. Behavioural Hypotheses
Hypothesis	Potential measurable behaviour
Transaction uncertainty creates confusion	Transaction-related support queries
Beginners struggle with terminology	Education and help-centre usage
Uncertainty about performance affects confidence	Performance-related support interactions
Financial constraints affect SIP continuity	Pauses and failed instalments
Reliable explanations help resolve confusion	Self-service resolution rate
6. Correlation Versus Causation

Observed associations between engagement and SIP continuation do not establish causality.

A controlled experiment would be required to estimate the incremental effect of a product intervention.

7. Measurement Limitations
Internal Groww customer-level data is unavailable.
No actual 12-month continuation rate is known.
No causal relationship has been established.
Proposed behavioural indicators require validation.
8. Next Steps
Validate behavioural hypotheses through primary research.
Prioritise opportunities using evidence.
Define leading indicators relevant to the selected solution.
Develop an experimentation plan.
